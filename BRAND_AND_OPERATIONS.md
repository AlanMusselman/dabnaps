## 2. In-House Platinum Silicone Casting SOP (100% Self-Made)

Developing our silicone mats, pads, and custom grips 100% in-house gives Dabnaps full control over our signature **Honey Gold, Caramel Bronze & Obsidian** swirl, heat resistance, and durometer softness without relying on any external factories.

### Material Specification
- **Base Polymer:** Two-Part (1:1 Mix Ratio) **Platinum-Cured Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR)** — Shore 45A to 60A.
- **Compliance:** FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 Food/Medical Grade, BPA/BPS-free, zero peroxide by-products, odorless.
- **Thermal Rating:** Continuous service up to **500°F (260°C)**.
- **Chemical Resistance:** 100% resistant to **99% Isopropyl Alcohol** cleaning soaks without swelling or pigment bleed.

### 5-Step In-House Production Workflow
1. **In-House Mold Prep (`3D-Printed PLA+/PAHT-CF` or `Milled 6061`):** Clean in-house 3D-printed ironed molds or milled cavities using isopropyl alcohol.
2. **Batch Weighing & Golden Pigment Swirl:** Weigh Part A & Part B (1:1 by weight). Mix base shade (**3% Honey Gold `#FFB800`**), and fold in **1.5% Caramel Bronze `#8C5300`** and **Obsidian Carbon Black** for the signature Dabnaps golden swirl.
3. **Vacuum Degassing:** Place mixed silicone in vacuum chamber at **29 inHg for 6–8 minutes** until air bubbles completely collapse.
4. **Pour & Primary Cure:** Pour steadily from a single high corner in a thin stream to vent air. Cure at ambient or in heated chamber.
5. **Post-Cure & QC:** Demold, trim any flash, and bake in a **Post-Cure Convection Oven at 392°F (200°C) for 4 hours** to lock in Shore 45A–60A hardness and 500°F thermal stability.

## 3. In-House 2× Bambu Lab P2S Additive Fabrication Standards

All structural frames, session docks, mold boxes, and elastomer bumpers are printed 100% in-house on our **2× Bambu Lab P2S Combo** farm (`0.4 mm` Hardened Steel Nozzle, `300°C` max nozzle, `110°C` max bed):

1. **Standard Structural Frames & Docks (`Bambu PETG-CF` — P2S #1 AMS Slot 1):** Rigid matte carbon-fiber frames for modular desk docks and silicone mat trays.
2. **High-Temp / High-Impact Structural Carbon (`Bambu PAHT-CF / PA12-CF` — P2S #1 AMS Slot 2, dried `<12% RH`):** High heat-deflection thermal rests, heavy-duty cradles, and high-temp silicone curing molds.
3. **Silicone Pour Molds, Master Patterns & Jigs (`eSUN PLA+ / Bambu PLA Tough` — P2S #1 AMS Slot 3):** Precision ironed mold boxes (`0.12 mm` layer height + top surface ironing) with zero platinum-cure inhibition.
4. **In-House Elastomers (`Bambu TPU 95A HF` on P2S #2 External Dryer Port + `Bambu TPU for AMS 68D` in P2S #1 AMS Slot 4):**
   - **NEVER put soft `TPU 95A` inside the AMS** (feed `TPU 95A HF` from P2S #2's external dryer port, sliced at `2 walls + 15% gyroid infill` for soft shock-absorbing feet and coasters).
   - Use **`Bambu TPU for AMS (68D)`** inside P2S #1's AMS for multi-color snap-fit lids and Golden/Obsidian composite badges.
