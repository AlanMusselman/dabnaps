In-House Platinum Silicone Lab

🧪 Capability 01 • In-House Platinum Silicone Casting

Printed-Tooling & 500°F Platinum-Cured Silicone Lab.

Every silicone piece we create is designed in CAD, poured into our own 3D-printed ironed molds or milled tooling, vacuum-degassed at 29 inHg, and post-cured at 392°F in-house. We use FDA food/medical-grade two-part Platinum-Cured Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR) with zero fillers—meaning zero off-gassing, zero swelling in 99% isopropyl alcohol, and continuous thermal stability up to 500°F (260°C).

1

In-House Mold Tooling

We print precision negative mold boxes on our Bambu Lab P2S (`0.12mm` layer height with top-surface ironing in `PLA+` or high-temp `PAHT-CF`) with zero platinum catalyst inhibition.

2

29 inHg Vacuum Degassing

1:1 Part A & Part B platinum silicone is hand-swirled with Radiant Dab Gold, Honey Wax Amber, and Obsidian pigments, then degassed at 29 inHg for 6–8 minutes to eliminate micro-bubbles.

3

Laminar Pour & 4h Post-Cure

After curing and demolding, every silicone casting undergoes a 4-hour 392°F post-cure oven cycle to lock in Shore 45A–60A durometer and 500°F thermal endurance.

4

Hybrid Carbon-Frame Integration

Cured silicone mats and rests drop directly into recessed pockets of our 3D-printed `Bambu PETG-CF` and `PAHT-CF` modular desktop frames.

🖨️ Capability 02 • 2× Bambu Lab P2S Combo Farm (`0.4mm` Hardened Steel)

Master 4-Tier Filament Standard for 100% Self-Made Hardware.

Our in-house additive farm runs a strictly standardized 4-tier polymer & elastomer discipline across two Bambu Lab P2S Combo printers (`300°C` max nozzle, `110°C` max bed)—allowing us to fabricate structural carbon-fiber frames, high-temp cradles, silicone pour molds, and soft shock bumpers completely in-house.
