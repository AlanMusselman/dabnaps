🧪 Dabnaps In-House Silicone Engineering

From CAD Mold to Platinum-Cured Perfection.

Unlike generic mass-imported mats, every Dabnaps Silicone Tray is designed, degassed, poured, and post-cured in-house. We use FDA food/medical-grade two-part Platinum-Cured Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR) with zero fillers — meaning it won't off-gas, discolor in 99% isopropyl alcohol, or warp under hot quartz tools.

1

CNC 6061 Aluminum Tooling

We mill custom bead-blasted aluminum molds with precision pockets for 14mm/10mm bangers, cotton swabs, iso vials, and terp jars.

2

29 inHg Vacuum Degassing

Part A & Part B platinum silicone is hand-swirled with Radiant Dab Gold, Honey Wax Amber, and Obsidian pigments, then vacuum-degassed to eliminate micro-bubbles.

3

Thermal Cure & 4h Post-Cure

Every tray undergoes a 4-hour 392°F post-cure oven cycle to lock in Shore 50A hardness and 500°F thermal stability.

4

Laser Serialization & QC

Each tray is inspected for flash, tested with 99% ISO, and logged directly into our live inventory tracker before hitting the storefront.
