## 3. Deep Technical Research: 100% In-House Self-Made Fabrication Stack

### 3.1 Process 1: Printed-Tooling Platinum Silicone Casting (`Shore 45A–60A`)
Instead of paying external CNC shops thousands of dollars for aluminum molds with 6-week lead times, we manufacture our own molds and cast our own silicone 100% in-house:
1. **3D-Printed Master Molds (`Bambu PLA+` / `PETG-CF`)**:
   - Printed on **Bambu Lab P2S #1** with a `0.4 mm` nozzle, `0.12 mm` layer height, `4 walls`, `25% gyroid infill`, and **Top Surface Ironing (`15% flow, 30 mm/s`)** to create glass-smooth mold cavities with embossed Golden Leaf & Cloud medallions.
   - Safe for platinum-cured silicone (unlike sulfur-containing clays or certain UV resins that inhibit platinum catalyst curing, pure `PLA+` and `PETG-CF` are 100% cure-compatible with Platinum LSR).
2. **Platinum LSR Formulation & Swirl Pigmentation**:
   - **Base Polymer**: 1:1 Mix Ratio Food/Medical-Grade Platinum-Cured Liquid Silicone Rubber (`Shore 45A` to `60A`), BPA/BPS-free, rated to **500°F (260°C)** continuous thermal exposure and **100% resistant to 99% Isopropyl Alcohol**.
   - **In-House Pigment Recipe**:
     - Base: `3.0% wt` Honey Wax Amber (`#FFB800`) silicone pigment
     - Accent Vein: `1.5% wt` Caramel Leaf Bronze (`#8C5300`) + `1.0% wt` Radiant Gold (`#FFD23F`) mica/pigment
     - Contrast Base: `2.0% wt` Obsidian Carbon Black (`#0A0A0A`)
3. **29 inHg Vacuum Degassing & Post-Cure**:
   - Degas mixed Part A + B at **29 inHg for 6–8 minutes** until bubble collapse completes.
   - Pour from a single high corner in a thin laminar stream; cure at ambient or `150°F` (for `PLA+` molds) / `175°F` (for `PAHT-CF` or aluminum molds), followed by a **4-hour post-cure at 392°F (200°C)** after demolding to lock in maximum tear strength and thermal stability.

### 3.2 Process 2: Multi-Material Additive Manufacturing (`2× Bambu Lab P2S Combo`)
Our in-house 3D printing farm follows the strict **4-Tier Filament Standard**:

| Printer & Feed Slot | Material | Key Engineering Properties | DabNaps Self-Made Applications |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P2S #1 — AMS Slot 1** | **Bambu PETG-CF** | Rigid carbon-fiber composite, low warp, matte carbon finish, chemical resistant | Modular desk organizer frames, silicone mat border trays, magnetic dock housings |
| **P2S #1 — AMS Slot 2** | **Bambu PAHT-CF / PA12-CF** *(Dried `<12% RH`)* | High heat deflection (`~140°F–194°F+`), extreme impact strength | High-temp thermal rests, high-temperature silicone curing mold cavities, heavy-duty cradles |
| **P2S #1 — AMS Slot 3** | **eSUN PLA+ / Bambu PLA Tough** | High dimensional accuracy, smooth surface ironing, zero platinum cure inhibition | Precision two-part silicone pour molds, alignment jigs, embossed logo test plates |
| **P2S #1 — AMS Slot 4** | **Bambu TPU for AMS (68D)** | Semi-rigid elastomer compatible with automated AMS multi-color switching | Multi-color Golden/Obsidian snap-fit lids, composite sandwich badges, wear-resistant skids |
| **P2S #2 — External Dryer Port** | **Bambu TPU 95A HF** *(Never in AMS)* | Flexible elastomer; sliced at `2 walls + 15% gyroid infill` to mimic softer `85A/90A` cushion | Non-slip shock-absorbing base bumpers, flexible jar coasters, protective corner boots |

### 3.3 Process 3: Hybrid Silicone + Carbon-Fiber Over-Assembly
By combining **Process 1** and **Process 2**, we build hybrid creations that no generic drop-shipper can replicate:
- **Rigid-Soft Modular Docks**: A structural **`Bambu PETG-CF` or `PAHT-CF` outer frame** printed with dovetail interlocks and recessed pockets, paired with a removable **in-house poured 500°F Platinum Silicone insert** and **`TPU 95A HF` anti-slip base pads**.
- **100% Zero-Vendor Independence**: Every single part is CAD-modeled, printed, poured, cured, and assembled under our own roof.
