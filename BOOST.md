## 1. Core Strategic Mandate (Founder's Law)
1. **Website Only — Zero Products for Sale**:
   - Do **not** list products for sale on `dabnaps.gg`.
   - Strip all retail catalog grids, pricing, shopping carts ("Cloud Bag"), and checkout drawers.
2. **100% In-House Self-Made Only**:
   - Everything showcased on `dabnaps.gg` must be something we design, 3D-print, mold/cast, or build **ourselves in-house**.
   - **Zero outsourced items**: No overseas cut-and-sew blankets, no vendor-sourced glass rigs, no imported USB-C ceramic hot-knives, and no third-party retail goods.

## 2. Multi-Perspective Deep Thinking Audit

### Perspective A: Brand & Cultural Positioning
- **Insight**: Removing the e-commerce storefront transforms `dabnaps.gg` from a retail shop into an exclusive **In-House Maker Studio & `.GG` Golden-Hour Lounge**.
- **Design System**: Retain the unmistakable **Radiant Dab Gold (`#FFD23F`)**, **Honey Wax Amber (`#FFB800`)**, **Caramel Leaf Bronze (`#8C5300`)**, and **Obsidian Black (`#0A0A0A`)** visual identity with *Fraunces* and *Plus Jakarta Sans* typography.

### Perspective B: In-House Fabrication & Equipment Alignment
- **Insight**: Our in-house hardware stack consists of:
  1. **Platinum-Cured Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR) Casting Lab**: 1:1 two-part medical/food-grade Shore 45A–60A platinum silicone, `29 inHg` vacuum degassing chamber, and `392°F` post-cure oven (`500°F` service rating).
  2. **2× Bambu Lab P2S Combo Farm (`0.4 mm` Hardened Steel Nozzle)**:
     - **P2S #1 AMS Slot 1**: `Bambu PETG-CF` (structural docks, trays, organizer frames)
     - **P2S #1 AMS Slot 2**: `Bambu PAHT-CF / PA12-CF` (high-temp thermal rests & structural cores, dried `<12% RH`)
     - **P2S #1 AMS Slot 3**: `eSUN PLA+ / Bambu PLA Tough` (silicone mold boxes, embossed master patterns, alignment jigs)
     - **P2S #1 AMS Slot 4**: `Bambu TPU for AMS (68D)` (multi-color semi-rigid badges, snap lids, composite inlays)
     - **P2S #2 External Dryer Port**: `Bambu TPU 95A HF` (never placed in AMS; sliced at `2 walls + 15% gyroid infill` for soft shock-absorbing feet & protective bumpers)
  3. **In-House Web & CAD Engineering**: Custom SVG blueprints, interactive web tools, and brand graphics built 100% internally.
